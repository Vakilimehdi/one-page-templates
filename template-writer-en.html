About

A writer who believes slowness is a skill

I write about the small, deliberate choices that make a life feel like your own: mornings without a phone, notebooks that outlive apps, and work done slowly on purpose. My essays have appeared in The Common Reader, Field Notes, and The Slow Review.

Before writing full-time, I spent eight years as a magazine editor. I live in Portland, Oregon, with two cats and a garden that mostly survives on neglect. My first book, The Unhurried Hour, is out now.
